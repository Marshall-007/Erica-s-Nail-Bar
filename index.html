Our story

Your nails,
your confidence.

Erica's Nail Bar is a small, appointment-only studio built on one idea: that a beautifully finished set changes how you carry yourself. Every set is shaped, sculpted and painted by hand, from a quiet nude overlay to marble, chrome and 3D florals.

Bring an inspiration picture and we'll design around it together during your booking consultation, so the set that comes off the lamp is exactly the one you pictured.

  • Gel X specialistsSoak-off extensions that stay light on the natural nail.
  • Custom hand-painted artMarble, blooms, croc, foil, charms and 3D detail.
  • Housecalls availableWe can come to you, charged separately.

Good to know

Booking policy

A few house rules that keep every appointment calm, unhurried and on time.

  • A 50% deposit secures your appointment.The balance is settled on the day.
  • Book at least 2 days in advance.It gives you the best chance of your preferred date.
  • Send your inspo design at consultation.Before the appointment, so everything is ready.
  • A late fee applies after 10 minutes.Arriving on time protects the client after you.
  • No-shows are cancelled.Missing an appointment without letting us know cancels it.
  • Housecalls are charged separately.Ask on WhatsApp and we'll quote for your area.
